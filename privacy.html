Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Personlig-tasks is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Tasks, using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.
  • The login token is stored locally on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected from, shared with or sold to anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
